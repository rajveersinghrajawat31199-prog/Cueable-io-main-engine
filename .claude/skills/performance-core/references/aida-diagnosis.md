# Reading results: AIDA diagnosis (creative levers)

Source: Demand Curve's Meta module ("AIDA framework", "How to test ad creatives"), grade **O** (practitioner method, no dataset cited). It is the most useful link between results and what we should change in a VIDEO. It works for any platform whose reporting exposes the equivalent numbers: **verify each platform's metric names in the account** (LinkedIn's are not confirmed here).

| Stage | Question | Metric (their formula) | Weak signal means | Creative lever (what we change) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Attention** | Does the hook stop the scroll? | thumb-stop rate = 3-second plays / impressions | Low: nobody stays past the first seconds | New hook, new first frame, new persona callout. Their experience: aim for at least 10% (grade O, "seen as high as 57%") |
| **Interest** | Does it hold attention? | average watch time, completion, drop-off curve (needs about 1,000 plays on Meta) | Big drop at a specific second | Cut or reorder at that second: too slow to the point, long headline, dead beat |
| **Desire** | Do they want the next step? | link click-through rate (clicks on the CTA specifically) | Watched but did not click | Different offer or CTA; a clearer ask; proof earlier |
| **Action** | Do they convert? | cost per result / cost per lead | Clicks but no leads | Mostly OUTSIDE the video: landing page continuity, form length, offer. Keep the same characters/message on the page |

Rules from the same source (all **O**):
- There is no universal benchmark; establish a baseline across your own first ads, compare metrics between ads, not to an industry number.
- Test several ads at once, radically different concepts, then iterate on the winning theme.
- Do not decide on one run: auctions move, so relaunch losers later; a winner today fatigues over months.
- Cost per result should stay under about one third of customer lifetime value (their rule of thumb).
- The landing page should build on the ad, not be the homepage.

Use in the studio: every results reading ends with ONE creative change per weak stage, written as a new variant in the same naming scheme (`...-v2`).
